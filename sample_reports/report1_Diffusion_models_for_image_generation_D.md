## Introduction to Diffusion Models
Diffusion models are a class of deep learning models that have gained popularity in recent years for their ability to generate high-quality images from text prompts. These models work by iteratively refining a random noise signal until it converges to a specific image that represents the input text prompt [3]. The process involves a series of transformations that progressively refine the input noise signal, allowing the model to learn complex patterns and relationships between the input text and the output image. Diffusion models have been shown to be highly effective in image generation tasks, producing images that are often indistinguishable from real-world images [4]. The importance of diffusion models in image generation lies in their ability to generate images that are not only visually appealing but also contextually relevant to the input text prompt.

The definition of diffusion models is rooted in the concept of diffusion processes, which involve the gradual refinement of a random noise signal over time. In the context of image generation, diffusion models use a series of transformations to refine the input noise signal, allowing the model to learn complex patterns and relationships between the input text and the output image [5]. The capabilities of diffusion models in image generation are vast, ranging from generating simple images to complex scenes and objects.
